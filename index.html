About me

I am a postdoc in economics at the Technical University of Berlin since September 2023. I am endlessly fascinated by human behaviour, and my research focuses on social norms, polarisation, and fairness. As I delve into these topics, I admire the synergies between the behavioural disciplines, which enable us as economists to understand and model how people act and think.

I am a co-organiser of the Berlin Behavioral Economics Seminar Series, and I am an affiliated researcher at the Berlin School of Economics, the Berlin Centre for Consumer Policies and the Copenhagen Network of Experimental Economists.
